About me

Exploring mathematical structures through knots, links, and topology.

I am Danish Ali, a mathematician whose work is centred on knot theory and low-dimensional topology. I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematical Sciences at Dalian University of Technology (DUT), China, in December 2023.

My research has examined invariants of classical and virtual knots and links, skein relations involving virtual crossings, and algebraic structures for studying dichromatic links. I am also interested in computational approaches that support the development and testing of knot-theoretic ideas.
